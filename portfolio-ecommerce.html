E-commerce Platform

Full-Featured Online Store

Project Overview

Developed a complete e-commerce platform for a retail business looking to expand their online presence. The platform includes a comprehensive product catalog, shopping cart, secure payment processing, and admin dashboard for managing inventory and orders.

The solution was designed to handle high traffic volumes, provide a seamless shopping experience, and integrate with multiple payment gateways. It features advanced search functionality, product filtering, customer accounts, and order tracking capabilities.
